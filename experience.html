Beyond the Track: Your Kenyan Adventure

Discover the vibrant community, culture, and unforgettable moments that make KHPTC more than just a training camp.

A Day at KHPTC Community Meetups

Karibu Sana! (You Are Most Welcome)

KHPTC welcomes athletes from over 25 countries each year. Whether you're chasing a marathon PB, recovering from injury, or simply seeking a unique travel experience, you'll find a home here among the red dirt roads of Eldoret.

A Day in the Life at KHPTC

Follow the rhythm of a typical day at the centre — from sunrise strides to evening stories around the fire.

6:00 AM

Sunrise Strides on the Track

As the first light breaks over Kazi Mingi Farm, athletes gather on the red clay track. The cool morning air at 2,400m altitude is perfect for intervals — this is where champions are forged.

30–60 min session
7:30 AM

Farm-to-Table Breakfast

After the morning session, enjoy a hearty breakfast prepared by our nutrition team. Fresh eggs, local fruits, and Kenyan chai — all sourced from the surrounding farmland.

Performance nutrition
10:00 AM

Strength & Conditioning

Hit our fully equipped gym for a guided strength session. From Olympic lifts to core stability, our coaches tailor the workout to your running goals.

All levels welcome
1:00 PM

Rest, Recovery & Connection

Afternoons are for rest and connection. Take a nap in your hostel room, get a massage, or join fellow athletes in the common area for coffee and conversation.

Community time
5:00 PM

Evening Long Run on Moiben Road

As the afternoon heat fades, it's time for the legendary long run. The matatu follows with water, gels, and even a photographer to capture the moment.

Matatu support included
7:00 PM

Dinner & Stories by the Fire

The day winds down with a communal dinner. Share stories with athletes from around the world, learn from local legends, and plan tomorrow's training together.

Bonfire nights

Community Meetups & Social Events

Connect with fellow runners, coaches, and local legends through our weekly gatherings and special events.

Sunday Community Run

Every Sunday at 7 AM, join runners from the centre and local clubs for a relaxed 10–15 km social run through the farm trails.

Bonfire Story Nights

Twice a week, gather around the fire to share stories with elite athletes, coaches, and fellow international visitors.

Coaches' Corner

Weekly Q&A sessions with resident coaches — bring your questions about training, nutrition, or racing strategy.

Cultural Exchange Dinners

Monthly themed dinners where trainees share dishes and traditions from their home countries with the KHPTC family.

Free Time & Local Adventures

When you're not training, explore the beauty and culture of Uasin Gishu County and beyond.

Eldoret City Tour

Explore the "City of Champions" — visit the Kipchoge Keino Sports Complex, local markets, and historic sites.

Farm & Nature Walks

Stroll through Kazi Mingi Farm's lush fields, spot local birdlife, and enjoy the peaceful Rift Valley landscape.

Fitness & Crossfit in Eldoret

Take a 30-minute trip to Rupaz Fun n Fitness for a swim, boxing session, or full crossfit workout.

Weekend Safari Options

Ask our team about day trips to nearby attractions or a weekend safari to Lake Nakuru or Maasai Mara.
