The Legacy of Kipchoge Keino

The man behind the centre — an Olympic legend who turned his farm into a home for champions

2× Olympic Gold Medalist

Kipchoge Keino

One of the greatest distance runners in history, Kipchoge Keino won Olympic gold in the 1500m at Mexico City 1968 and the 3000m steeplechase at Munich 1972 — inspiring generations of Kenyan athletes.

After retiring, Keino dedicated his life to nurturing young talent. In 2002, he established the Kip Keino High Performance Training Centre on his family farm — Kazi Mingi — to give promising athletes from Kenya and beyond a place to train, live, and grow into champions.

Today the centre is recognized by the International Olympic Committee and hosts refugee athletes, national teams, and visitors from around the world.

Getting Here

Easy access from Eldoret International Airport — just 17.5 km to Kazi Mingi Farm

Travel Time

30–35 min

Average travel time depending on traffic. A smooth, scenic drive through the high-altitude ridges.

Direction

Northeast

The farm sits roughly northeast of the airport terminals, on the scenic ridges just outside Eldoret town.

Turn-by-Turn Route from Eldoret International Airport

1

Leave Eldoret Airport

Exit the terminal and follow Airport Path away from the airport grounds.

2

Connect Northeast

Join the Eldoret – Kapsabet Road / Kisumu Road (C39) heading northeast.

3

Pass Elgon View

Continue east past the Elgon View area, enjoying views of the high-altitude landscape.

4

Take Plateau Road (B54)

Turn onto Plateau Road (B54) and follow it right up to the Kazi Mingi Farm compound.
