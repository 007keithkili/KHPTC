Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common inquiries

Where exactly is KHPTC located?
KHPTC is located on Kazi Mingi Farm, just outside Eldoret town in Uasin Gishu County, approximately 350 km west of Nairobi, Kenya.
Do I need to be a professional athlete to train there?
No! About 90% of our visitors are leisure runners. We welcome athletes of all levels, from beginners to elite competitors.
What is the best time of year to visit?
The centre is open year-round. September to April generally offers the mildest weather, but training is excellent in all seasons.
Can I arrange airport transfers?
Yes, we offer transfers from Eldoret International Airport and Nairobi. Just let us know your flight details when booking.
Is the centre suitable for non-runners?
Absolutely! Family members and non-running companions are welcome. There are plenty of activities and excursions available.
