The Legacy of Kipchoge Keino

Discover the story behind Africa's premier high-performance training centre – where Olympic dreams are nurtured on the red soil of Eldoret.

2× Olympic Gold 1968 & 1972

Kipchoge Keino

Olympic Champion | Founder & Visionary

Kipchoge Keino is one of the greatest distance runners in history. He won Olympic gold in the 1500m at Mexico City 1968 and the 3000m steeplechase at Munich 1972, inspiring generations of Kenyan athletes.

After retiring, Keino dedicated his life to nurturing young talent. In 2002, he established the Kip Keino High Performance Training Centre on his family farm — Kazi Mingi — to provide world-class training facilities for promising athletes from Kenya and beyond.

The centre has since become an IOC-recognized High Performance Training Centre, listed by the IAAF, and a home for middle and long-distance champions, refugee athlete scholarship holders, and national teams from around the world.

Our Journey

Key milestones in the KHPTC story

2002

The Centre Opens

Kipchoge Keino establishes the High Performance Training Centre on Kazi Mingi Farm, just outside Eldoret, to provide elite training facilities for Kenyan athletes.

2008

IAAF Recognition

The centre is officially listed as a High Performance Training Centre by the International Association of Athletics Federations (IAAF).

2012

Olympic Success

Athletes trained at KHPTC represent Kenya at the London Olympics, winning multiple medals in middle and long-distance events.

2016

IOC Recognition

KHPTC receives formal recognition from the International Olympic Committee (IOC) as an elite training facility, expanding its international reach.

2019

Refugee Athlete Program

The centre partners with World Athletics and UNHCR to host refugee athlete scholarship holders, providing training and education opportunities.

2023

Continued Growth

KHPTC expands its facilities with upgraded residential hostels, a modern gym, and enhanced links to the Kipchoge Keino Sports Complex in Eldoret.
